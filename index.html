How to Play

The mission

Zap is a 4–8 player hidden-role space battle. Each person plays on their own phone. The Commander is public; every other role stays secret until that player is eliminated. Every player also receives a face-up Pilot with a public special ability.

Secret roles

🪐 CommanderStay alive and eliminate every Raider and the Rogue Unit.
🛡️ SentinelSecretly protect the Commander. You share the Commander’s victory.
☄️ RaiderEliminate the Commander. Raiders win together unless the Rogue is the only survivor.
🤖 Rogue UnitBecome the final surviving player.

Face-up pilots

Roles are secret, but Pilot cards are public. Tap any Pilot name on the crew table to read that Pilot's printed health and special ability. The Commander receives 1 health above the Pilot's printed health.

Your turn

  • You automatically draw two cards.
  • Tap a card to use it. Cards that do not need a target play immediately.
  • For a target card, the players you can reach receive a thick rose-colored double outline. Tap an outlined player card, then tap the small confirmation button such as “Zap?”
  • Play as many cards as you want. Normally you may use one Zap attack per turn; certain Pilots and mission events increase that limit.
  • On each crew card: ♥ is health, the stacked cards show hand size, 🎯 is firing range, and 📏 is distance from you. A thick cyan double outline marks whose turn it is. The command patch marks the public Commander; unrevealed roles show a question mark.
  • Your normal firing range is 1 seat. The players immediately beside you are distance 1; players farther around the circle are distance 2, 3, and so on. Weapons and some Pilot abilities increase how far you can fire.
  • Cards you do not play stay in your hand. When you end your turn, your hand limit equals your current health. For example, at 3 health you can keep 3 cards; Zap automatically discards random extras.

Cards

The × number shows how many copies are added to the deck. Expansion cards are included only when their module is selected.

Eliminations

A Deflector in your hand automatically blocks one point of damage. Eliminating a Raider earns three cards. If the Commander accidentally eliminates a Sentinel, the Commander loses every card and equipped item.
