How to Play

The mission

Zap is a 4–8 player hidden-role space battle. Each person plays on their own phone. The Commander is public; every other role stays secret until that player is eliminated.

Secret roles

🪐 CommanderStay alive and eliminate every Raider and the Rogue Unit.
🛡️ SentinelSecretly protect the Commander. You share the Commander’s victory.
☄️ RaiderEliminate the Commander. Raiders win together unless the Rogue is the only survivor.
🤖 Rogue UnitBecome the final surviving player.

Your turn

  • You automatically draw two cards.
  • Tap a card to use it. Cards that do not need a target play immediately.
  • For a target card, the players you can reach glow red. Tap a red player card, then tap the small confirmation button such as “Zap?”
  • Play as many cards as you want, but only one Zap attack per turn.
  • On each crew card: ♥ is health, the stacked cards show hand size, 🎯 is firing range, and 📏 is distance from you.
  • Your normal firing range is 1 seat. The players immediately beside you are distance 1; players farther around the circle are distance 2, 3, and so on. Weapons increase how far you can fire.
  • End your turn when finished. Extra cards above your current health are automatically discarded.
  • Bots pause for about five seconds after each action so everyone has time to read the mission log.

Cards

Eliminations

A Deflector in your hand automatically blocks one point of damage. Eliminating a Raider earns three cards. If the Commander accidentally eliminates a Sentinel, the Commander loses every card and equipped item.
